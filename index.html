◉Project Filaments

Air gap: parts are stacked with a gap of layers × layer height. The slicer leaves that space empty, so each part rests on a thin air layer and pulls apart by hand. BambuStudio will say the file has an invalid config and load geometry only; that's expected, click OK.

Interface layers: there is no air gap: the space is filled with a slab shaped like the part's top face, assigned to a second filament. Pick two materials that don't bond (PLA parts + PETG interface, or the other way round) and they separate cleanly. Great with an AMS.

In BambuStudio: make sure the project has a filament in the interface slot, match the layer height you entered here, then slice and check the preview.
